Before the release

A practical checklist for pipeline readiness, verification, ownership and rollback.

A successful pipeline run is one part of a release. The surrounding work matters just as much: agreeing the change, understanding its dependencies and deciding how to respond when verification fails.

Before a change window, review the release scope, configuration, access and dependencies. Name the person responsible for the deployment and the people who can assess application behavior.

  • Before deployment: confirm the artifact, configuration, permissions and rollback procedure.
  • During deployment: watch the pipeline and the service state, with a clear owner for decisions.
  • After deployment: verify health checks, application behavior, logs and relevant metrics.
  • Rollback: define the trigger and rehearse the steps before they are needed.

Adapt the checklist to the risk of the system. A small static site and a stateful production application need different levels of verification.
