FiatLine

A personal finance assistant inside LINE, designed around everyday income and expense tracking.

FiatLine is a personal finance assistant delivered as a LINE mini-app.

The project explores how an everyday workflow can fit into a familiar messaging environment, with an application backend and its deployment managed together.

  • Bring income and expense tracking into LINE.
  • Connect an external platform with an application backend.
  • Consider the operational needs of the application from the start.
