Korvuz Atlas

A private travel planner for LINE, with shared itineraries, trip collaboration and controlled sharing.

Korvuz Atlas, presented in the app as Korvuz Trip, brings trip planning and a personal travel archive into LINE.

The owner can invite Editors or Viewers to a specific trip. Separate expiring, read-only links let selected trips be shared without granting app membership.

  • Plan itineraries and collaborate on ideas and checklists.
  • Keep journals, photos and personal travel history under the owner's control.
  • Operate a Next.js application backed by PostgreSQL.
