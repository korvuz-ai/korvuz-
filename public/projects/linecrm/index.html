LineCRM

Customer management inside LINE Official Account, built for Thai retailers with Next.js and PostgreSQL.

LineCRM connects customer management with the LINE Official Account channel already used by Thai retailers.

The project combines a Next.js application, PostgreSQL and messaging integration, with responsibility for deploying and operating the service.

  • Design application and database workflows together.
  • Integrate with an external messaging platform.
